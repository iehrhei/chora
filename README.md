# CHORA

*χώρα* — a wall in the Ouvroir de Rhei, and an oval mirror that sometimes lets the deep sea in.

Live sea by courtesy of Schmidt Ocean Institute, Ocean Exploration Trust, NOAA Ocean Exploration,
and the NSF Ocean Observatories Initiative / University of Washington (Axial Seamount HD camera).
The figure in the O is drawn after an Attic red-figure cup of the fourth century BC.

The mirror shows visitors only in their own browser; nothing is recorded or sent.
Words left on the wall stay in the visitor's own browser.
